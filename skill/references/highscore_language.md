# 高级词汇与高级语法升级库（批改时的"提分工具箱"）

> **使用原则（先读这三条）**
> 1. **正确优先于高级**：升级句必须语法正确、搭配地道；没把握就用原句，不硬升级。
> 2. **升级要克制**：一篇 160—200 词的作文，**有效升级 3—5 处**即可；10 处以上容易变成"炫技"，反而降档。
> 3. **按需取用**：只在这个位置本来就需要这类表达时才升级，不要为了用高级词而改意思。
>
> 报告里给升级建议时统一写成：`原句 → 升级句（其中 X 处替换说明）`，标注"可选优化"，并附"没把握时的退路句"。

## 一、高级词汇升级库

> 表格第三列标注：**搭配**（必须和谁连用）、**语域**（正式/中性/偏口语）、**可数性**（n. 是否可数）。这是替换时最容易出错的地方。

### 1.1 描述图画／图表

| 普通表达 | 高级表达 | 使用注意 |
|---|---|---|
| `The picture shows...` | `The drawing vividly portrays...` | portray 及物，直接接宾语；比 show 有画面感，适合首段首句 |
| `We can see a man in the picture` | `The drawing features a young man who...` | feature 作动词意为"以…为特色"，主语须是图画/作品，不能是人 |
| `The chart tells us that...` | `The chart reveals that...` / `The data point to...` | reveal 后接从句；data 是复数，point to 用复数动词 |
| `As is shown in the chart` | `As the chart makes clear, ...` / `As can be seen from the chart, ...` | 后者 as 在从句中作 see 的宾语，不能写成 As can be seen from the chart shows |
| `The number is 45%` | `The figure stands at 45%.` / `... accounts for 45% of the total.` | stand at 用于数值；account for 主语须是"部分" |
| `The picture is simple but meaningful` | `The drawing is deceptively simple yet thought-provoking.` | deceptively simple = 看似简单实则不然，别理解成"欺骗性的" |
| `The picture has a deep meaning` | `The drawing carries a symbolic message.` | deep meaning 是中式搭配，改用 symbolic message / implied message |
| `The chart shows a big change` | `The chart captures a marked shift in...` | marked = 显著的，书面；shift in + 名词 |
| `In the picture, two people are talking` | `The picture presents two people engaged in conversation.` | be engaged in + n./doing，固定搭配 |
| `The picture describes a common scene` | `The picture mirrors a scene familiar to many of us.` | mirror 作动词"反映"，书面 |
| `The figures are very different` | `The gap between the two figures is striking.` | striking = 引人注目的；gap between A and B |
| `The chart is about...` | `The chart focuses on...` / `The chart is concerned with...` | be concerned with = 关于，不是"担心"（那是 be concerned about） |

### 1.2 表达原因

| 普通表达 | 高级表达 | 使用注意 |
|---|---|---|
| `because` | `owing to / due to + 名词或动名词` | 严格语域下 due to 不接从句；接从句用 because / since |
| `because of this` | `for this reason` / `hence` | hence 书面，后可直接接名词或句子 |
| `The reason is that...` | `The root cause lies in...` | lie in 后接名词；接从句须写 lies in the fact that |
| `causes` | `gives rise to` / `contributes to` | 两者 to 均为介词，后接名词或 doing，不接动词原形 |
| `Many reasons cause this` | `A combination of factors is responsible for this.` | be responsible for 亦可表"是…的原因"，主语多为 factors |
| `First... Second...` | `The primary driver is... A further factor is...` | driver 此处指"驱动因素"，可数 |
| `mainly because` | `largely because` / `chiefly because` | 后接从句，位置灵活 |
| `leads to` | `results in` / `brings about` | result in 主语是原因；result from 主语是结果，方向别反 |
| `This is why...` | `This explains why...` | 后接完整从句 |
| `due to the fact that` | `given that` / `considering that` | 后接从句，书面，可放句首 |
| `People do this because they want...` | `The urge to ... drives people to ...` | drive sb. to do sth.，及物 |
| `The problem comes from...` | `The problem stems from...` | stem from 主语是结果，from 后接原因 |

### 1.3 表达影响

| 普通表达 | 高级表达 | 使用注意 |
|---|---|---|
| `affects` | `has a bearing on` / `exerts an influence on` | have a bearing on 中 a 不可省；influence 此处可数 |
| `is bad for` | `is detrimental to` / `does harm to` | detrimental 后接 to；harm 不可数 |
| `is good for` | `is beneficial to` / `works to the advantage of` | beneficial to sb./sth. |
| `makes ... better` | `improves` / `enhances` | enhance 及物，后面不加 to |
| `makes people worry` | `gives cause for concern` / `arouses public concern` | concern 此处不可数 |
| `results in` | `entails` / `brings about` | entail = 必然伴随（代价、风险），后接名词 |
| `very important` | `plays a vital role in` / `is central to` | central to + n.，不接从句 |
| `changes` | `transforms` / `reshapes` | 均及物，语气强，适合"根本性改变" |
| `helps` | `facilitates` / `contributes to` | facilitate 及物，不接 to do |
| `causes problems` | `poses a threat to` / `puts pressure on` | pose a threat to 固定搭配 |
| `in the long run` | `over time` / `in the long term` | 三者可互换，over time 更中性 |
| `does damage to` | `takes a toll on` | 只用于负面影响，toll 可数（a toll） |

### 1.4 表达对比

| 普通表达 | 高级表达 | 使用注意 |
|---|---|---|
| `but` | `whereas` / `while` | 二者引导从句，须两侧都有主谓；不能像 but 那样连接两个名词 |
| `different` | `differ markedly` / `stand in sharp contrast to` | differ 不及物，differ from / in |
| `more than` | `outnumber` / `exceed` | outnumber 主语、宾语都须是复数群体 |
| `compared with` | `in comparison with` / `relative to` | 后接名词；relative to 书面 |
| `on the other hand` | `conversely` / `by contrast` | 表"相反"，不是"另一方面"，别混用 |
| `the same` | `share a common feature` / `bear a resemblance to` | resemblance 可数，常用 a resemblance to |
| `however` | `nevertheless` / `even so` | 书面；nevertheless 后可加逗号 |
| `twice as many` | `twice the number of` / `double the figure for` | twice the number of + 复数名词 |
| `A is bigger than B` | `A far exceeds B in...` | exceed 及物，in 后接比较维度 |
| `not only ... but also` | `not merely ... but also` | 连接成分须平行；not merely 放句首时主句倒装 |
| `instead` | `rather than` / `in place of` | rather than 连接平行成分，前后形式一致 |

### 1.5 表达观点

| 普通表达 | 高级表达 | 使用注意 |
|---|---|---|
| `I think` | `In my view` / `From my perspective` / `I would argue that` | 后两者书面；argue that 后接完整从句 |
| `I agree` | `I am inclined to agree that` / `I share the view that` | inclined to 语气留有余地，适合"部分同意" |
| `Many people think` | `It is commonly held that` / `There is a widespread belief that` | 后者 belief 可数，用 a |
| `Some people say` | `Critics argue that` / `Proponents maintain that` | maintain 后接 that 从句，书面 |
| `In my opinion` | `To my mind` / `As I see it` | 中性偏口语，作文可用但别每段都用 |
| `I think it is wrong` | `I find it hard to accept that...` | 后接从句，语气缓和但立场清楚 |
| `obviously` | `It stands to reason that` / `Clearly` | 前者 = 按理说，逻辑推断，不宜滥用 |
| `I believe` | `I am convinced that` / `I hold the view that` | convinced 语气强于 believe |
| `it is important` | `It matters a great deal that...` | matter 作动词，主语是 that 从句时用形式主语 it |
| `we should` | `It is high time that we...` | 从句用**过去式**（took），这是硬规则 |
| `I don't think` | `I doubt whether...` / `I am not persuaded that...` | doubt 在肯定句中接 whether，不接 that |

### 1.6 表达建议

| 普通表达 | 高级表达 | 使用注意 |
|---|---|---|
| `should` | `ought to` / `would do well to` | would do well to do sth. = 最好做某事，书面 |
| `must` | `It is imperative that ... (should) do ...` | 从句用动词原形（虚拟），不加 -s |
| `suggest` | `recommend that ... (should) do ...` / `propose that` | 同上，从句用原形 |
| `we need to` | `There is a pressing need to...` | pressing = 紧迫的，后接不定式 |
| `government should` | `The authorities should` / `Policymakers need to` | authorities 表"当局"用复数 |
| `try to` | `make a concerted effort to` | concerted = 协同的，后接不定式 |
| `pay attention to` | `attach importance to` / `give priority to` | 两者 to 均为介词，后接名词或 doing |
| `solve` | `address` / `tackle` / `remedy` | 三者均及物，不接 to；remedy 偏"补救" |
| `stop` | `curb` / `rein in` | curb 及物；rein in 可分开用（rein in spending） |
| `let people know` | `raise public awareness of` | awareness 不可数 |
| `improve` | `strengthen` / `upgrade` | strengthen 多接制度、能力；upgrade 多接设施 |
| `in my opinion we should` | `It would be advisable to...` | 委婉建议，适合书信与结尾段 |

### 1.7 表达趋势

| 普通表达 | 高级表达 | 使用注意 |
|---|---|---|
| `increase` | `rise` / `climb` / `grow` | 三者均不及物，不能带宾语 |
| `increase quickly` | `surge` / `soar` | 语气强，只用于**大幅**上涨；小幅用 edge up |
| `decrease` | `decline` / `fall` / `drop` | 均不及物；decline 亦可作名词 |
| `decrease quickly` | `plunge` / `plummet` | 只用于暴跌，数据平缓时用会失真 |
| `stay the same` | `remain stable` / `level off` / `plateau` | level off 强调"由升转平"；plateau 作动词书面 |
| `change a lot` | `fluctuate wildly` | fluctuate 不及物，常与 between A and B 连用 |
| `gradually` | `steadily` / `progressively` | steadily 强调匀速，progressively 强调递进 |
| `reach the highest point` | `peak at` / `hit a peak of` | peak at + 数值，主语是数据 |
| `reach the lowest point` | `bottom out at` / `hit a low of` | bottom out 强调"触底（后将回升）" |
| `from 2010 to 2020` | `over the decade` / `between 2010 and 2020` | over the decade 前不加 the past 也可 |
| `double` | `double` / `more than double`；`triple` | 作动词时及物、不及物均可：The figure doubled. |
| `continue to rise` | `continue its upward trend` / `keep climbing` | upward trend 可数，用 its/the |

### 1.8 表达结论

| 普通表达 | 高级表达 | 使用注意 |
|---|---|---|
| `In conclusion` | `To conclude` / `In summary` / `All things considered` | 三者都放段首，后加逗号；一篇文章只用一次 |
| `so` | `therefore` / `consequently` / `as a result` | consequently 后常加逗号 |
| `we can see` | `It follows that` / `This suggests that` | 后接完整从句 |
| `finally` | `Ultimately` / `In the final analysis` | 表"归根结底"，不是"最后一步" |
| `in a word` | `In short` / `Put simply` | Put simply 后加逗号 |
| `generally speaking` | `By and large` / `On the whole` | 均放句首，后加逗号 |
| `it is clear` | `It is evident that` | evident 比 clear 书面 |
| `this shows` | `This points to` / `This goes to show that` | 后者略口语，作文优先前者 |
| `we should remember` | `It is worth bearing in mind that` | bear in mind 固定搭配，不写 bear in mind of |
| `in the future` | `Looking ahead` / `In the years to come` | 段首独立使用，后加逗号 |
| `only in this way` | `Only in this way can we...` | 句首 Only + 状语，主句**部分倒装** |

### 1.9 表达重要性／必要性

| 普通表达 | 高级表达 | 使用注意 |
|---|---|---|
| `important` | `crucial` / `vital` / `indispensable` | indispensable 后接 to/for；语气最强 |
| `necessary` | `essential` / `imperative` | imperative 多作表语：It is imperative to... |
| `play a role` | `play a pivotal role in` | pivotal = 关键的；role 用 a |
| `cannot be ignored` | `cannot be overstated` / `should not be overlooked` | cannot be overstated = 怎么强调都不为过 |
| `key` | `a cornerstone of` / `a prerequisite for` | 均可数，须加 a |
| `everyone knows` | `Few would dispute that` | 双重否定表强调，书面 |
| `it is useful` | `it serves a practical purpose` | purpose 可数，用 a |
| `have to` | `cannot afford to` | 后接动词原形，语气更重 |
| `matters` | `counts` / `carries weight` | count 此处不及物 = 重要 |
| `basic` | `fundamental` / `underlying` | underlying = 深层的，多修饰 cause/problem |

### 1.10 描述问题／危害

| 普通表达 | 高级表达 | 使用注意 |
|---|---|---|
| `serious` | `grave` / `acute` / `mounting` | grave 多修饰 consequence/problem；mounting 表"不断加剧的" |
| `problem` | `challenge` / `dilemma` / `defect` | dilemma 指两难，不要泛用为"问题" |
| `worry` | `anxiety` / `concern` | anxiety 多不可数；concern 可数/不可数均可 |
| `get worse` | `deteriorate` / `be aggravated` | deteriorate 不及物，不能带宾语 |
| `many` | `a growing number of` | 后接可数名词复数 |
| `harm` | `undermine` / `erode` | 均及物；erode 强调"逐渐侵蚀" |
| `waste` | `squander` | 及物，多接机会、资源、时间 |
| `lack` | `a shortage of` / `a dearth of` | dearth 书面且极正式，一篇最多一次 |
| `difficult` | `daunting` | 多修饰 task/challenge，表"令人却步的" |
| `cause trouble` | `create friction` / `give rise to conflict` | friction 不可数 |

## 二、高级语法结构清单（阅卷加分项）

> 每种给：公式 → 考研语境例句 → 适用位置 → 常见误用。**一篇作文用 4—6 种即可**，全用会显得匠气。

### 2.1 非谓语·doing 作状语／定语

- **公式**：`Doing sth., 主句`（状语，逻辑主语＝主句主语）／`n. + doing sth.`（定语，主动、进行）
- **例句**：
```
Living in an age of information overload, we are constantly tempted by distractions.
```
- **位置**：首段引入背景、主体段分析原因
- **误用**：悬垂分词——`Walking on the street, the advertisement caught my eye.`（走路的是"我"，不是广告）→ `Walking on the street, I noticed an advertisement.`

### 2.2 非谓语·done 作状语／定语

- **公式**：`Done / 形容词化的过去分词 + 主句`／`n. + done`（被动、完成）
- **例句**：
```
Compared with previous generations, today's graduates face a far tougher job market.
```
- **位置**：首段对比、主体段给结论
- **误用**：把 done 当谓语用，或逻辑主语与主句主语不一致——`Faced with pressure, the problem...` 主语错位；应写成 `Faced with pressure, many young people...`

### 2.3 非谓语·to do 作定语／目的状语

- **公式**：`n. + to do`（定语，常表"将要/应当"）／`To do sth., 主句`（目的）
- **例句**：
```
To curb online addiction among teenagers, schools and parents need to work together.
```
- **位置**：首段点题、尾段提建议
- **误用**：目的状语与主句主语不一致；`for to do` 不存在，目的只能用 to do 或 in order to。

### 2.4 非限制性定语从句 which 指代整句

- **公式**：`..., which + 谓语 ...`（which 指代前面整件事，不用 that）
- **例句**：
```
The number of private cars has risen sharply, which has worsened traffic congestion in big cities.
```
- **位置**：主体段承接结果、尾段评价
- **误用**：用 that 引导非限制性从句；which 与先行词距离过远导致指代不清。

### 2.5 定语从句 whose / where

- **公式**：`n. + whose + n. + 谓语`／`n. + where + 完整句`（where = in which）
- **例句**：
```
The report focuses on children whose parents work in cities far from home.
```
```
We need a society where honest work is respected and rewarded.
```
- **位置**：首段描述对象、主体段举例
- **误用**：where 后接不完整句（where 从句必须主谓宾齐全）；whose 指物时与 of which 混用尚可，但别写成 whose 引导的从句缺主语。

### 2.6 名词性从句·what 主语从句

- **公式**：`What + 主语 + 谓语 + is ...`（what 在从句中充当成分，不能换成 that）
- **例句**：
```
What the chart reveals is a widening gap between urban and rural incomes.
```
- **位置**：首段概括图表、尾段亮观点
- **误用**：`What matters most is...` 写成 `That matters most is...`；what 从句后误加 it。

### 2.7 名词性从句·whether 同位语从句

- **公式**：`n.（question / issue / doubt）+ whether + 完整句`
- **例句**：
```
The question whether online education can replace classroom teaching remains open.
```
- **位置**：首段引出争议、主体段转述对立观点
- **误用**：同位语从句用 if（if 不能引导同位语从句）；whether 与 or not 位置写乱（whether or not 可整体使用，但别出现 if ... or not）。

### 2.8 状语从句·让步（however / no matter how）

- **公式**：`However + adj./adv. + 主语 + 谓语, 主句`／`No matter how + adj./adv. + ...`
- **例句**：
```
However advanced technology becomes, it cannot replace human judgment.
```
- **位置**：主体段让步后转折、尾段升华
- **误用**：`However advanced technology is` 后仍加 but（英语不允许 however...but 并用）；however 后缺形容词直接接句子。

### 2.9 条件倒装（Were it not for / Had ... / Should ...）

- **公式**：`Were it not for + n., 主句`／`Had + 主语 + done, 主句`／`Should + 主语 + do, 主句`
- **例句**：
```
Had the government acted earlier, the damage would have been far smaller.
```
```
Were it not for public transportation, commuting in big cities would be almost impossible.
```
- **位置**：主体段假设论证、尾段警示
- **误用**：倒装后仍加 if；`Had it not been for` 与现在事实混用（与过去事实才用 had been）。

### 2.10 部分倒装（Only when / Not until / So ... that / Hardly ... when）

- **公式**：`Only + 状语 + 助动词 + 主语 + 谓语`；`Not until ... + 助动词 + 主语 + 谓语`；`So + adj./adv. + 助动词 + 主语 + 谓语 + that ...`
- **例句**：
```
Only when we learn to say no can we focus on what really matters.
```
```
Not until we lose our health do we realize how precious it is.
```
```
So rapidly has the sharing economy grown that it now touches almost every aspect of daily life.
```
- **位置**：尾段呼吁、主体段强调因果
- **误用**：缺助动词（Only when... we can → can we）；So...that 倒装时 that 从句语序不必倒装。

### 2.11 强调句 It is ... that ...

- **公式**：`It is/was + 被强调成分 + that/who + 其余部分`
- **例句**：
```
It is consistent effort, rather than sudden inspiration, that makes a difference.
```
```
It was not until 2010 that the city began to build subway lines.
```
- **位置**：尾段点题、主体段给判断
- **误用**：强调人时可用 who，但强调时间/地点只能用 that；it is...that 中 that 一般不可省；原句谓语动词不能整体被强调。

### 2.12 虚拟语气（If ... were to ... / It is high time that ...）

- **公式**：`If + 主语 + were to do, 主语 + would do`（将来假设）；`It is high time that + 主语 + 过去式`
- **例句**：
```
If the government were to raise the tax on plastic bags, consumers would think twice before using them.
```
```
It is high time that we took concrete action to protect the environment.
```
- **位置**：主体段提对策、尾段呼吁
- **误用**：high time that 后用动词原形（应为过去式，或 should + 原形）；were to 后接动词原形。

### 2.13 独立主格

- **公式**：`名词/代词 + doing / done / 形容词 / 介词短语, 主句`（自带逻辑主语，不是主句主语）
- **例句**：
```
His homework finished, the boy rushed out to play football.
```
```
Weather permitting, the sports meeting will be held as scheduled.
```
- **位置**：首段场景描写、主体段交代条件
- **误用**：独立主格与主句之间用逗号连接却另加 and/so；逻辑主语与主句主语混同（那就该用普通分词）。

### 2.14 with 复合结构

- **公式**：`With + 宾语 + doing / done / 介词短语 / 形容词, 主句`
- **例句**：
```
With the number of elderly people rising, demand for care services is growing fast.
```
```
With more and more people shopping online, traditional stores are struggling to survive.
```
- **位置**：首段背景、主体段因果
- **误用**：with 后接完整句子（应接"宾语＋补足语"）；doing 与 done 选错（主动用 doing，被动用 done）。

### 2.15 平行结构

- **公式**：`A, B and C` 三项词性/形式完全一致；`not only ... but also ...` 两侧对称
- **例句**：
```
Reading broadens our horizons, sharpens our thinking and enriches our inner life.
```
- **位置**：尾段总结、主体段列举理由
- **误用**：`to reduce costs, improving efficiency and protect the environment` 三种形式混杂；not only 后接从句而 but also 后接名词。

### 2.16 插入语

- **公式**：`主语, + 插入成分 + , 谓语 ...`（however entertaining / in my view / to some extent）
- **例句**：
```
Short videos, however entertaining, can consume hours of our time.
```
```
The government, in my view, should play a bigger role in regulating the platform economy.
```
- **位置**：首段评价、主体段亮立场
- **误用**：插入语前后缺逗号导致句子结构断裂；插入语过长（超过 8 词）打断主谓。

### 2.17 比较结构 the more ... the more ...

- **公式**：`The + 比较级 + 主语 + 谓语, the + 比较级 + 主语 + 谓语`
- **例句**：
```
The more we rely on smartphones, the less we trust our own memory.
```
- **位置**：主体段说理、尾段收束
- **误用**：比较级前漏 the；后半句误用陈述语序导致结构失衡（`the less we trust...` 为正常语序，不必倒装，但不要把 trusted 之类时态写乱）。

### 2.18 其他比较结构（far more ... than / nothing is more ... than）

- **公式**：`far/much + 比较级 + than`；`Nothing is more + adj. + than + n.`
- **例句**：
```
Far more college students choose to work in big cities than in their hometowns.
```
```
Nothing is more important than health.
```
- **位置**：首段对比、尾段强调
- **误用**：比较对象不对等（`The income of farmers is lower than city workers` → than that of city workers）。

## 四、常见失分点清单

### 4.1 语言类（10 条）

| 错误示例 | 正确示例 | 一句话规则 |
|---|---|---|
| `The number of private cars have risen sharply.` | `The number of private cars has risen sharply.` | `the number of + 复数名词`作主语，谓语用**单数**（a number of 才用复数）。 |
| `Last year, the figure increases to 30%.` | `Last year, the figure increased to 30%.` | 有明确过去时间状语，谓语必须用过去时。 |
| `He is a honest man.` | `He is an honest man.` | 冠词看**读音**不看字母，h 不发音用 an。 |
| `We should pay attention to our behaviours.` | `We should pay attention to our behaviour.` | behaviour 表"行为表现"时不可数，别加 -s。 |
| `Walking on the street, the advertisement caught my eye.` | `Walking on the street, I noticed an advertisement.` | 分词状语的逻辑主语必须等于主句主语。 |
| `The book which I borrowed it is interesting.` | `The book which I borrowed is interesting.` | 关系词已在从句中作宾语，不能再加 it。 |
| `We should discuss about the problem.` | `We should discuss the problem.` | discuss 及物，不加 about；同类还有 enter / marry / reach。 |
| `I very like this picture.` | `I like this picture very much.` | very 不能直接修饰动词，修饰动词用 very much / really。 |
| `The environment pollution is serious.` | `Environmental pollution is serious.` | 修饰名词用形容词（environmental），不用名词。 |
| `With the development of society, ...` | `As society changes, ...` | 该短语已被用滥且信息量为零，换成具体变化。 |

### 4.2 结构类（9 条）

| 错误示例 | 正确示例 | 一句话规则 |
|---|---|---|
| 段落无主题句，直接举例 | `Two factors may account for this trend.` 后接例证 | 每段首句必须是**可被论证的判断句**。 |
| 两段都在描述图画，无人分析 | P1 描述、P2 归因、P3 评论 | 三段各有唯一功能，功能重叠即结构失分。 |
| 连接词堆砌：`Firstly... Secondly... Moreover... Finally...` 一段全用 | 一段用 1—2 个，靠内容衔接 | 衔接靠逻辑，不靠连接词数量。 |
| 小作文写成一大段 | 称呼／正文 2—3 句／结尾＋落款 | 应用文按"块"分段，不按"意思"分段。 |
| 首段占全文一半 | 首段不超过全文 1/3 | 首段任务是"引入＋点题"，不是展开。 |
| 结尾重复首段原句 | 首段提问题，尾段给判断 | 结尾要"升级"，不是"复读"。 |
| 只有观点没有例证 | 观点＋一句具体现象/数据 | 每个主体段至少一个可核实的支撑。 |
| 投诉信开头就指责，求职信通篇 `I want` | `I have always been a loyal customer, which is why...` | 语气与身份匹配：投诉要克制，求职要专业。 |
| 图表作文只描述数据、不概括趋势 | `Overall, the decade witnessed a marked shift...` | 图表题必须有"总体判断句"。 |

### 4.3 内容类（9 条）

| 错误示例 | 正确示例 | 一句话规则 |
|---|---|---|
| 只描述图画，漏掉 `give your comments` | 末段写 `In my view, ...` | Directions 的每个动词都是一个得分点，逐条对照。 |
| 通篇 `With the development of economy and society` | `As online payment becomes part of daily life, ...` | 空话不产生内容分，具体才产生。 |
| 论据与论点无关（谈环保却举学习成绩） | 论点"塑料污染"，例证"超市塑料袋用量" | 例证必须能直接支撑该段判断。 |
| 把百分比当人数 | `45% of respondents` 而非 `45 respondents` | 读图先分清"比例/数量/增长率"。 |
| 编造图中没有的细节 | 只写图上可见的元素 | 描述题"所见即所写"，编造按偏题处理。 |
| 前段说"利大于弊"，尾段说"应当禁止" | 全文立场一致 | 立场可以有限定，但不能自相矛盾。 |
| 建议信只写建议，不写理由与期待 | 建议＋一句理由＋一句期待回复 | 应用文三要点：目的、内容、回应方式。 |
| 邀请信不写时间地点 | `at 7 p.m. on Friday, 12 June, in the main hall` | 邀请/通知类缺时间地点＝信息不全。 |
| 字数明显不足（英语一 <150 词） | 控制在 170—190 词 | 字数不足先扣"内容不完整"，再扣语言。 |

**教研备注（批改时可直接引用）**
1. 语言分的提升顺序是：**先消灭 4.1 的十类硬错 → 再练 2—3 个语法结构 → 最后才换高级词**。顺序颠倒会写出"高级词＋基础错"的怪文章。
2. 模板痕迹的本质是"句子与题目无关"。判断标准很简单：把模板句里的名词换掉后，这句话是否还成立？成立＝模板痕迹重。
3. 英语一 2024 起图表题与图画题并存，训练时两种都要写；只练图画会在考场上直接失分。

## 五、升级建议的输出模板（批改报告里照此写）

```
【可选优化】原句：The picture shows many students are using phones.
升级句：The drawing depicts a group of students absorbed in their phones,
        with their heads bent over the screens.（with 复合结构，1 处升级）
理由：首段描述用 depict 更准确；with 结构把"低头看屏幕"的画面收进一句，避免两个简单句连排。
提醒：如果对 with 结构没把握，用 The drawing depicts a group of students who are absorbed in their phones. 同样得分。
```

**硬性要求**：每条升级建议必须附"没把握时的退路句"，避免学生硬背高级句反而写错。
